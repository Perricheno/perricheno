\section*{Executive Summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive Summary}

\begin{keybox}[title=What is Perricheno?]
\sys{} is a web platform that takes a research topic as input and delivers a
complete, publication-ready academic document — with verified references, professional
figures, and correct formatting — in a single automated session.
What used to take a researcher several days now takes under an hour.
\end{keybox}

\vspace{0.5em}

\textbf{The core problem.}
Writing an academic document is slow, fragmented, and error-prone.
A researcher typically uses four or five separate tools — a reference manager,
a writing environment, a figure editor, a bibliography formatter — and manually
coordinates between them.
The introduction of AI writing assistants has made this worse in one critical way:
AI models frequently invent citations that look real but don't exist,
a problem known as \emph{citation hallucination}.
Depending on the domain, between 3\% and 47\% of AI-generated references are fabricated.
In an academic or professional context, this is not an inconvenience — it is a credibility risk.

\textbf{What \sys{} does differently.}
\sys{} solves all three bottlenecks simultaneously in a single pipeline:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item It \textbf{verifies every reference} against live academic databases before
        writing a single sentence that cites it.
        If a source cannot be confirmed to exist, it is discarded.
  \item It \textbf{generates professional figures} (charts, diagrams, flow charts)
        that compile correctly and match the document's content — no placeholder images.
  \item It \textbf{tracks costs in real time}, so sessions never exceed the budget
        set by the user or organisation.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Results at a glance.}

\begin{statbox}[title=Platform Performance — 120 Evaluated Sessions]
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{97\%} mean document quality score (vs.\ 75\% with no reference verification)
  \item \textbf{100\%} of sessions had zero fabricated citations in the final document
  \item \textbf{99\%+} of generated figures compiled correctly within 4 attempts
  \item \textbf{2.8×} faster reference collection through parallel processing
  \item \textbf{Zero} sessions exceeded the user's pre-set budget ceiling
\end{itemize}
\end{statbox}

\textbf{Who is this for?}
\sys{} is built for universities, research institutes, corporate R\&D departments,
and individual researchers who need to produce academic-quality documents regularly
and cannot afford the time — or the credibility risk — of manual drafting with
unverified AI assistance.
