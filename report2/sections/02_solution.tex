\section{The Solution: What Perricheno Delivers}
\label{sec:solution}

\subsection{A Single Session, A Complete Document}

\sys{} is built around one core promise: give us your topic and your requirements,
and we hand you back a complete, compilable academic document — with verified references,
embedded figures, and correct formatting — without you having to touch any other tool.

A ``complete document'' in the \sys{} context means:

\begin{itemize}
  \item A structured academic text with all standard sections (introduction,
        methodology, results, discussion, conclusion, or whatever structure you specify).
  \item A verified bibliography containing only sources that exist and are accessible
        at the time of generation — no invented citations.
  \item Professional TikZ/pgfplots figures embedded in the document and cross-referenced
        correctly in the body text.
  \item Correct \LaTeX{} formatting that compiles to a clean PDF without manual intervention.
  \item A billing record showing exactly what the session cost, broken down by stage.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Five Guarantees}

\sys{} is built around five properties that hold for \emph{every} session by design,
not just on average.
These are not aspirational targets — they are structural guarantees that follow from
how the system is built.

\begin{keybox}[title=The Five Perricheno Guarantees]
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \textbf{No duplicate formatting.}
        Every generated document has exactly one properly formatted header.
        No structural duplication, no conflicting style definitions.

  \item \textbf{Zero fabricated citations.}
        Every reference in the bibliography was fetched from a live academic database
        at the time of generation.
        If a source cannot be verified to exist, it is excluded — period.

  \item \textbf{Predictable costs.}
        The cost of a session increases as you add more content and figures,
        and never decreases. This means a budget ceiling set before the session
        is guaranteed to hold.

  \item \textbf{Correct section order.}
        Even when sections are written in parallel to save time, the final document
        always presents them in the order you specified in the outline.
        Section 3 always comes after Section 2.

  \item \textbf{Live sources only.}
        References are not pulled from a cache or a snapshot of the internet from
        months ago. Every source is checked to be live and accessible
        at the moment your session runs.
\end{enumerate}
\end{keybox}

\subsection{What the Output Looks Like}

The output of a \sys{} session is a \LaTeX{} source bundle that compiles to a PDF.
The PDF contains:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2em, style=nextline]
  \item[Structured prose.]
    Section-by-section academic text, written in the language you specify (English,
    Russian, Kazakh Cyrillic, or Kazakh Latin), at the word count you request.
    The prose is grounded in the verified sources — the system writes \emph{about}
    real papers, not about invented ones.
  \item[A verified bibliography.]
    Every reference is formatted in the citation style you choose (IEEE, APA, GOST,
    Chicago) and resolves to a live DOI, URL, or institutional handle.
  \item[Professional figures.]
    Charts (bar, line, scatter, histogram), flow diagrams, architecture diagrams,
    and other visual types — generated programmatically and embedded in the document
    with correct figure numbers and captions.
  \item[Clean compilation.]
    The document compiles without error on a standard \LaTeX{} installation.
    You do not need to manually fix bibliography errors, undefined cross-references,
    or figure compilation issues.
\end{description}
